Basic Info

London is the capital and largest city of England and the United Kingdom, with a population of 9.14 million people from 2025. It is the U.K.'s largest metropolis and its economic, transportation, and cultural center.London has over 32 boroughs including City of London: The historic and financial centre (the "Square Mile"), which is a separate entity and not a standard borough. London is a vibrant global capital where ancient royal customs meet a diverse, modern multicultural arts scene.

London has different Boroughs and Areas which are spread across:

  • North London
  • North-West London
  • North-East London
  • East London
  • West London
  • South-West & South-East London

London offers world-class historic landmarks, free museums, and vibrant neighbourhoods to explore. Some of these places have been in movies and are iconic as of today and places that represent the history and culture of London.
